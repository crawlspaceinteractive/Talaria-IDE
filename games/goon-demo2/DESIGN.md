## GOBLIN DUNGEON - Retro FPS
Wolfenstein/DOOM-inspired 2.5D raycaster with infinite procedurally generated dungeon levels.
Core mechanic: DDA raycasting engine, kill all enemies to advance; every 5th level is a TROLL WARLORD boss, every 25th is THE ABOMINATION. Post-boss shop for weapons (shotgun, crossbow, cannon, sword, double plasma blaster) and upgrades. Separate gold/score resources.
Art style: 90s VGA aesthetic, pixelated 8-bit textures, flickering torchlight, dithered floors/ceilings. Animated enemy spritesheets (walk/attack per type), per-weapon held sprites & unique fire SFX, per-enemy death SFX, seed-based ambience pitch shifting.
