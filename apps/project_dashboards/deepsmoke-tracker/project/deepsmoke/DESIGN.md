# Deepsmoke

First-person voxel co-op drilling: you + AI goblin Grub descend a noise-generated steam-dungeon in fuel-hungry drill-suits, mining relics and ore, sharing fuel (throws/F-transfer), and hauling loot back up to the surface camp where the ORE VACUUM banks it — end the dig from your PACK before both suits lock. Bright Minecraft-style visuals in a brass-and-cobalt palette (#8B6914 #C4934A #D4B483 #1E3A8A #60A5FA).
